# Course Platform

A platform where instructors publish courses and students buy access to them and work through the lessons.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
A person with an enrollment on a course, however they obtained it. Being a student is relative to a course, not a property of the person.
_Avoid_: Learner, customer, buyer

**Owning Instructor**:
The one instructor a course belongs to.
_Avoid_: Author, teacher, course owner

### Learning

**Passed**:
A student has passed a quiz once any one of their attempts reached the quiz's passing score.
_Avoid_: Completed (for quizzes), solved

**Completed**:
A student has completed a course once they have completed every lesson in it and passed every quiz in it. Completion is permanent: later changes to the course do not undo it.
_Avoid_: Finished, done, graduated

### Access and money

**Enrollment**:
A student's access to one course. It exists whether the student paid for it themselves or redeemed a team seat.
_Avoid_: Membership, subscription, purchase

**Purchase**:
A payment made for a course, by a student for their own enrollment or by a team buyer for several seats. A purchase is not an enrollment and can exist without one.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction, enrollment

**Team Purchase**:
A purchase of several seats on a course, each of which another person can redeem for an enrollment. The buyer is not enrolled by it.
_Avoid_: Bulk order, group enrollment

**Team Seat**:
One place on a course bought through a team purchase, redeemable once by one person for an enrollment.
_Avoid_: Coupon, voucher, licence

**Total Paid**:
The money paid for a course that stayed with the company: all purchases for it, including team seats not yet redeemed, net of refunds.
_Avoid_: Revenue, earnings, gross, payout
